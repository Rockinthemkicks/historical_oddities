## Confirmed canon

- **C05** [HD, spoiler: none] GTA VI is set in the fictional state of Leonida, with Vice City as its central city.  
  Evidence: S07 (official, UNVERIFIED), S08 (official, UNVERIFIED), S23 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C06** [HD, spoiler: none] Rockstar's own description calls GTA VI's Vice City 'the darkest side of the sunniest place in America'.  
  Evidence: S07 (official, UNVERIFIED)
- **C07** [HD, spoiler: none] GTA VI's protagonists are Jason and Lucia.  
  Evidence: S07 (official, UNVERIFIED)
- **C08** [3D, spoiler: none] Vice City Stories is set in 1984, two years before Vice City.  
  Evidence: S04 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S10 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S12 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C10** [3D, spoiler: none] Victor 'Vic' Vance is a U.S. Army corporal stationed at Fort Baxter in Vice City.  
  Evidence: S04 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S10 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S12 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C11** [3D, spoiler: minor] Vic's sergeant, Jerry Martinez, runs a secret drug business, then frames Vic for drugs and for bringing a prostitute onto the base, and Vic is dishonorably discharged.  
  Evidence: S04 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S12 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S26 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C12** [3D, spoiler: minor] The cocaine Vic and Lance steal from Martinez turns out to belong to the Mendez brothers, Armando and Diego; Martinez was only moving it for them.  
  Evidence: S04 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S12 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S28 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C13** [3D, spoiler: none] In 1984 the Mendez brothers are the city's most powerful drug kingpins, while presenting themselves publicly as philanthropists.  
  Evidence: S04 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S28 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S14 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C14** [3D, spoiler: minor] Ricardo Diaz, a rival drug baron, employs the Vance brothers against the Mendez operation.  
  Evidence: S04 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S10 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S14 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C15** [3D, spoiler: major] After using the Vance brothers for a few jobs, the Mendez brothers betray them, and the Vances go to war with the cartel.  
  Evidence: S05 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S12 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S14 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C16** [3D, spoiler: major] The war costs Vic dearly: Louise dies after the Mendez brothers kidnap her, Armando is killed at the Mendez mansion, and Vic kills Diego and Martinez on the roof of the Mendez Building.  
  Evidence: S05 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S12 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S27 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C17** [3D, spoiler: none] Between 1984 and 1986 Diaz establishes himself as Vice City's dominant drug baron.  
  Evidence: S01 (primary_game, UNVERIFIED), S24 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C18** [3D, spoiler: none] Tommy Vercetti served fifteen years in prison for the killings in Harwood, Liberty City, and the press called him 'the Harwood Butcher'.

## Interpretation

- **C09** [3D, spoiler: none] The game's own framing presents 1984 Vice City as a boomtown whose growth is fueled by the drug trade, a city built on crime and betrayal.  
  Evidence: S18 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S10 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C25** [3D, spoiler: major] Lance's betrayal grew out of feeling underappreciated and sidelined in Tommy's rise; in the game he only calls it business.  
  Evidence: S11 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S21 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S29 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C36** [3D, spoiler: minor] Vice City's 1986 property boom was built in part on deliberately started gang violence.  
  Evidence: S30 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S31 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
- **C37** [3D, spoiler: major] Wiki accounts treat Sonny's earlier Harwood setup as fact; the game itself only implies it.  
  Evidence: S11 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S16 (secondary, UNVERIFIED), S29 (secondary, UNVERIFIED)
